    Questions students ask

    Who can join?

    Any college student or fresh graduate with a verified email address.

    How does the discount work?

    Your score on the 15-question test maps to a discount set by our admins. The final price is shown before you pay.

    How do team internships work?

    A leader creates a team and invites up to four registered students. The team shares one project and each member earns a separate certificate.

    How do I get my certificate?

    Once every mandatory task is approved, it is generated as a PDF, saved to your account, and emailed to you.

    Is the certificate verifiable?

    Yes. Every certificate has a unique ID that anyone can check on our public verify page.

    What if my task needs changes?

    You get written feedback, update your work, and resubmit. Earlier versions stay in your history.
